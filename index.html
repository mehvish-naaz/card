ENID SINCLAIR

Enid Sinclair is a character from the Netflix series Wednesday, portrayed by actress Emma Myers. Enid is a bright and cheerful neighbor to Wednesday Addams, known for her colorful personality and energetic demeanor. She is introduced as a supportive friend who shares a complex relationship with Wednesday, often contrasting their contrasting personalities. Enid is a werewolf from California and has been a significant character in the series, with her friendship with Wednesday being a central theme. The character has been well-received by fans, and her role has been a breakout opportunity for Myers, who has since appeared in other projects.
